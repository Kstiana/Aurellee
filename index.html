A ritual for every strand.

01

Cleanse

Begin with a gentle wash. Work the shampoo into the scalp and let it rinse through the lengths.

02

Condition

Smooth conditioner from mid-lengths to ends. Comb it through and give it a couple of minutes.

03

Treat

When your hair wants more, add a mask or a few drops of oil. This is the step to slow down for.

04

Style

Prepare damp hair with a leave-in, and protect it before any heat.

05

Finish

Close with a touch of oil on the ends, and leave the rest to the day ahead.
