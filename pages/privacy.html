AURELLE is a demonstration brand created for a design and engineering portfolio. Products, prices, policies and wording on this website are illustrative and are not commitments from a real business.

What stays on your device

Your bag, saved items and recently viewed products are stored in your browser using local storage. This information is not sent to us and can be cleared at any time from your browser settings. We also keep a small flag in session storage so the welcome animation plays only once per visit.

Forms

The newsletter and contact forms send the details you type to our form service so we can reply. We ask for consent before you submit, and we only use your details for the purpose you gave them.

WhatsApp orders

When you press the WhatsApp button, your browser opens WhatsApp with a message containing your bag contents and any note you add. Sending it shares that message with us and with WhatsApp, which handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. No payment details are collected or needed.

Third-party services

To display the website we load fonts from Google Fonts, icons from a public content delivery network and photographs from Pexels. These services may receive your IP address and basic browser information when they serve those files.

Cookies and tracking

We do not use advertising cookies or analytics trackers on this website.

Your choices

You can clear your stored bag and saved items from your browser, ask us to delete a message you sent, or unsubscribe from emails at any time. Write to kstiana1@gmail.com.
