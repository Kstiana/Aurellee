AURELLE is a demonstration brand created for a design and engineering portfolio. Products, prices, policies and wording on this website are illustrative and are not commitments from a real business.

How ordering works here

Orders are sent as a request by WhatsApp. We then reply to confirm availability, delivery details and any costs before anything is arranged.

Delivery

Because AURELLE is a brand, no delivery regions, costs or timeframes are promised on this website. A live store would list the countries it ships to and the carriers it uses.

You are welcome to enquire from anywhere. We will tell you honestly what is possible for your location.

Returns

This sample policy does not create a returns commitment. A live store would state clearly which items can be returned, within what period and in what condition, and how refunds are handled.

Hygiene products and made-to-order items are often treated differently from other goods, and a live policy would say so up front.

Damaged or incorrect items

If anything ever arrived damaged or incorrect, message us with a photo and your order details so we can look into it.
