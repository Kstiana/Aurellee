Questions,answered.

Everything you might want to know before you order. Still unsure? Message us.

Products

Are these real products?

AURELLE is a demonstration brand. The products, descriptions and prices are illustrative.

How do I choose a size or length?

Each product page shows the options available. If you are unsure, use the WhatsApp button on the product page to ask before ordering.

Where can I see how to use a product?

Every product page has a how to use section, and the Hair Journal covers rituals, treatments and styling in more detail.

Orders

How do I order?

Add items to your bag, review them and press the WhatsApp button. This opens a message with your selection, which you send to us as an order request.

Is my order confirmed straight away?

No. A WhatsApp message is a request. An order is only confirmed when we reply and you agree to the details.

Do I pay on the website?

No payment is taken on this website. Any payment arrangements would be discussed with you on WhatsApp for a live store.

Shipping

Do you ship internationally?

Enquiries from anywhere are welcome. Because this is a demonstration, no delivery coverage is promised here.

How long will delivery take?

Delivery timing would be confirmed for your location when you send your request. No timeframes are shown as guarantees.

Where can I read more?

See the shipping and returns page for how we describe delivery in this demonstration.

Returns

Can I return something?

A live store would publish its returns terms clearly. In this demonstration, no return window is promised.

What if an item arrives damaged?

Message us right away with a photo so we can look into it. This sample policy does not create a commitment.

Are wigs and hair products returnable?

Hygiene products and made-to-order pieces are often treated differently. A live store would state this up front.

Customer Care

How can I contact you?

Use WhatsApp for quick questions, or send an email or the contact form for anything longer.

Is there an account system?

Not yet. The account icon is a placeholder and no account features are live.

How is my information used?

Your bag and saved items are stored only in your browser. Read the privacy policy for the full picture.
